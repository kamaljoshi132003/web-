## Question 1: How do you create a basic HTML5 page with a heading and a paragraph about a favorite hobby?
**Answer:**
```html
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
  <meta charset="UTF-8">
  <title>My Hobby</title>
</head>
<body>
  <h1>My Favourite Hobby</h1>
  <p>I love playing cricket. It keeps me fit and teaches me teamwork.</p>
</body>
</html>
```
