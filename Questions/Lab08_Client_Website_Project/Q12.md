## Question 12: How do you test mobile responsiveness with browser developer tools?
**Answer:**
1. In Chrome, press `F12`, then `Ctrl + Shift + M` to open the Device Toolbar.
2. Test viewport widths such as iPhone SE (375px), iPhone 14 (390px), iPad (768px), and laptop (1366px).
3. Check for horizontal scrolling, readable text, touch targets around 44px, images overflowing the viewport, and broken navigation.
4. Capture a screenshot at each viewport. Include `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">` in every page.
