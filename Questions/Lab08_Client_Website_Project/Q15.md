## Question 15: How can you demonstrate the completed website in two to three minutes?
**Answer:**
1. Open `index.html` and show the carousel's automatic transitions and controls.
2. Scroll to the menu and team sections.
3. Submit invalid booking data to show an error, then submit valid data to show a confirmation.
4. Narrow the browser window or use `F12` device mode to demonstrate the responsive layout.
5. Press `Tab` to demonstrate visible keyboard focus.
6. Show the map on the contact page.
7. Present the Lighthouse report.

Rehearse the full demonstration in advance.
