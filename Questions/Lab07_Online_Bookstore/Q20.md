## Question 20: How do you test the complete bookstore customer and administrator workflow?
**Answer:**
| Step | Action | Expected result |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Open `index.php`. | The banner and featured books appear. |
| 2 | Search for `Clean`. | Matching books appear. |
| 3 | Open a book and select **Add to Cart**. | The cart count increases. |
| 4 | Enter a quantity greater than the available stock. | A warning appears and the quantity is limited. |
| 5 | Open checkout while signed out. | The browser redirects to the login page. |
| 6 | Register and sign in. | The home page appears with a logout link. |
| 7 | Enter shipping details and place an order. | The invoice page opens. |
| 8 | Open `orders.php`. | The new order appears with a `Pending` status. |
| 9 | Change the order status to `Shipped` as an administrator. | The customer sees the updated status. |
| 10 | Check `books.stock` in the database. | Stock has decreased by the quantity ordered. |
